How it works

Three additive mechanisms, no change to the CUDA rasteriser.

AESLAdaptive Edge-Structured Loss — modulates the photometric penalty with a Sobel edge prior.
HEM-SHard Example Mining Sampler — half the densification views drawn from the highest-loss cameras.
ADSAnnealed Densification Schedule — split interval decays 100 → 250 → 500.
